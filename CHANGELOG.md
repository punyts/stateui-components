Dragging maps the pointer to a value through the track width from a `ResizeObserver`; thumbs, marks and the fill are positioned with the custom properties `--sui-value`, `--sui-from` and `--sui-to`. `src/shared/slider/slider.ts` holds the scale, snapping and key helpers; the `min`, `max` and `step` rules of `createField` now judge each number of a list value.
- `disclosure` molecule (E8.36): a titled section that opens and closes. `open` (default false, reflected), `icon` (empty draws the built-in chevron, turned while closed), `disabled`, content `_title` and `_summary` (shown only while closed), the slots `children` (with `layout`) and `actions`, tokens `padding` and `gap`, event `toggle` (`{ open }`), commands `open`, `close` and `toggle`, German overlay. The header is a button with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`; a user toggle writes `open`.
- `stepper` molecule (E8.6): `steps` (a keyed map with `_label` and `_description`), `active`, `completed` (keys), `linear` (default true), `orientation`, content `_label` and `_completed`, tokens `gap`, `marker.size` and `selected`, event `select` (the key), command `complete` (adds a key to `completed`; used because `libraries/stateui-app` has no `append` system command), German overlay. An ordered list with `aria-current="step"` on the active step; a number or a check per step; with `linear`, steps after the first incomplete one are disabled. `src/shared/steps/steps.ts` (`planSteps`, `currentStep`, `keysOf`).
- `tabs` gets `strip` (`visible` default, `hidden`; reflected): a hidden strip is not drawn or focusable, and the panels still switch through `active` and `select` (E8.26).
- `wizard` composed component (E8.26): a `panel` with a `stepper` above `tabs` (`strip: "hidden"`) whose children are three step `form`s (`details`, `options`, `review`), each with Back and Next in its `actions` toolbar. Next submits its own form (`$parent`); a step's `submitted` runs `complete` on the stepper and selects the next step on the stepper (`set` `active`) and the tabs (`select`); the last step's `submitted` runs `emit wizard.done`. German overlay; exposes the step forms and the step labels. What `on` cannot express here is listed in the README (event payloads cannot be forwarded to a command, no relative target, no list removal, no merging of form values).
- `accordion` composite (E8.37, AMM-1777): a stack of `disclosure` children with `multiple` (default false), `open` (a key, or a list of keys with `multiple`), `bordered`, shared `density`, the event `change` and the commands `open`, `close`, `expandAll` (only with `multiple`) and `collapseAll`. It keeps its `open` and each child's `open` consistent whichever is written, and Up, Down, Home and End move focus between the headers. Its shape sets `childComponents: ["disclosure"]`, so a child of another component is `SUI-NODE-CHILD` (needs `@punyts/stateui-app` with the E8.37 change).
- `splitPane` composite (E8.7, AMM-1780): two panes (slots `start` and `end`) with a draggable divider. `orientation` (`horizontal`, `vertical`), `split` (default 40: the start pane's share in percent; named `split` because `size` is a reserved shared articulation name), `min` (20), `max` (80), `collapsible`, content `_label` and the event `resize` (`detail`: `{ split, collapsed }`). The divider is `role="separator"` with `aria-valuenow`, `aria-valuemin` and `aria-valuemax`; the arrow keys move it by 5%, Home and End go to `min` and `max`, and Enter collapses the start pane when `collapsible`. A drag converts pointer positions to a percentage through the container size from a `ResizeObserver`, updates only the `--sui-split` custom property while it runs and writes `split` once on pointer up, with one `resize`. Below the `md` breakpoint the panes stack and the divider is hidden. The pure parts are in `src/shared/split/split.ts`.
- `tree` composite, flattening and rendering (E8.8, AMM-1781): hierarchical records drawn through a `recordList` body. `key`, `parentField` (default `parentId`) or `childrenField` (a field holding the keys of the children), `hasChildrenField` (default `hasChildren`), `labelField`, `expanded` (keys), `indent` (`sm`, `md`, `lg`), data `records` and the `row` slot. The pure `flattenTree(records, options, expanded)` in `src/shared/records/tree.ts` gives the visible rows with their levels; it is written as the body's `order`, so expanding or collapsing re-points slots and rebuilds none. The depth of each record is the custom property `--sui-level` on its slot (the pool's new `slotLevel`, fed by the `meta` option and the element hook `slotMeta`); a cycle of parent keys hides its records and is reported once as `SUI-RUN-BINDING`. `recordList` gains the role `tree` (slots with `role="treeitem"`) and the element hook `invalidate`.
- `tree` composite, keyboard, selection and lazy children (E8.9, AMM-1782): the body has the role `tree` and each slot is a `treeitem` with `aria-level`, `aria-expanded`, `aria-setsize`, `aria-posinset` and an `aria-label` from `labelField`. Down, Up, Home, End, Right (open, or move to the first child), Left (close, or move to the parent) and typing a character (next record whose `labelField` starts with it) move focus; a press on a record that can open opens or closes it. `selectable`, data `selected` and the events `select` and `activate` use the shared selection code; the commands are `expand`, `collapse`, `expandAll` and `collapseAll`. `expand` is raised once for an open record marked by `hasChildrenField` that has no children loaded, and a busy row (a placeholder key in `order`, shown as a pending slot) stands under it until the children arrive. The pool's `slotMeta` now carries `expanded`, `setsize`, `posinset` and `label`, and the record view element gets `focusKey`.
- `chart` scale module (E8.11, AMM-1784): `src/shared/chart/scale.ts`, pure code with no DOM, locale or clock. `createLinearScale` and `createBandScale` (`domain` to `range`, with `invert`), `niceTicks(min, max, count, format?)` (1, 2 or 5 times a power of ten, covering the range, labels from the formatter the caller passes), `stack(series)` (positive values up and negative values down from 0, in series order), `extentOf` and `stackExtent`.
- `chart` composite, bar, line and area (E8.27, AMM-1785): an SVG chart over linked records, drawn with `createElementNS` in an `svg` with `role="group"` labelled by `_title` and described by `_description`. `type` (`bar` default, `line`, `area`), `x` (the record field of the category), `series` (a keyed map of `{ field, _label }`), `stacked`, `format` and `currency` (the shared formatter for `$.locale` labels the value axis), data `records` (an array or a keyed map), the token `height` (group `chart`). The size comes from a `ResizeObserver`; scales and ticks are E8.11; series colours are `color.data.categorical.1` to `8` in key order. A data change writes only the attributes that differ, so one changed value touches its own mark; a series, `type` or `stacked` change draws the chart again. Shared code: `src/shared/chart/geometry.ts` (`buildScene`: a pure list of SVG primitives with stable ids), `svgScene.ts` and `data.ts`.
